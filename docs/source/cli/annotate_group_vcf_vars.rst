.. rubric:: options

.. option:: -i ISOFORM_BED, --isoform_bed ISOFORM_BED, --bed_isoforms ISOFORM_BED

   bed file of isoforms whose exons name the sites by their genes, each its 13th column, as a flair isoform bed's, or, without it, the --gtf gene it overlaps most; as flair variantquant --isoform_bed

.. option:: -f GTF, --gtf GTF

   GTF annotation file, plain or gzipped, whose exons name the sites by their gene_ids, or, with --isoform_bed, the genes of its isoforms without them

.. option:: -v VCF, --vcf VCF

   vcf file, plain or gzipped, of the sites: of a database such as REDIportal or dbSNP, or called in the samples; only its first five columns are needed. Only SNVs of records whose FILTER is PASS or . are written, or one of --keep_flags

.. option:: --keep_flags FLAGS

   comma-separated vcf FILTER flags whose records are written too, or all, every record

.. option:: -o OUTPUT, --output OUTPUT

   the --pos_ref file to write, of flair variantquant's format
